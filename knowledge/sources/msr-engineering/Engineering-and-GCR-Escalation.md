**Escalation Alias:**  
Engineering <msritengineering@microsoft.com>
GCR <GCRAdmin@microsoft.com>

**Description**
There is a point person for engineering and for GCR. The point person is the engineer tasked with handling, on behalf of their respective teams, any new escalated issue not related to a current on-going deliverable. In effect, 
	• You respond to any escalated support requests submitted to the team. In particular you must monitor the contact alias noted above for support requests.
	• You shield the rest of the team both from email and investigation randomizations during your shift.
	• You are the point of contact to outsiders in need of assistance from the team for any new issue brought up during your shift.
	• If you are NOT the point person and you are contacted by someone for a new issue it is your responsibility to forward the request to the team alias and the point person specifically so that the point person can take ownership. If the point person is not available please use your judgement to determine if the customer’s issue is an emergency defined as business and time critical and requires Tier 2 support. If it meets this criteria you are empowered to assist the customer.

**Responsibility**
As the Point person you own: 
	• Investigating failures escalated to you from MSR Support or other sources of external issues. 
	• Acknowledge ownership of escalated issue thru email response CC'ing your engineering team within an hour.
	• Responses to questions should occur within 24 hours during business hours. Responses to higher urgency questions/mails should occur as promptly as possible. If you have any questions, please ask your lead. 

**Resolution**
	• You do not own providing a fix, but you may have to provide a work-around to unblock the other party.
	• You must CC the alias on all email conversations.
	• An acceptable resolution after an investigation would be to create a Bug in Azure DevOps (VSTS) if the effort was greater than 1 hour.
	• An unacceptable resolution would be to create a new bug without realizing that the issue is already known and filed.

**Assistance**
	• The expectation is NOT that the Point person to be an expert in all services and disciplines.
	• The expectation is that the Point person will investigate the issue as much as possible until he or she reaches an impasse. Only then is it acceptable to pull in the other engineers on the team. When this happens, the Point person has stopped being a shield for the rest of the team.
	• The only exception to this would be if a special time-constraint on the customer's side required the Point person to resolve an issue quicker than he or she possibly could.
	• The Point person remains at all times the contact for the external party that brought the issue to the team. Even when pulling in assistance from another engineer on the team, the Point person must own the communication with the other party. The helper should not have to exchange email with the other party directly. Remember, you're the shield when on duty.

**Transfer**
	• Transfer of the baton happens end of business hours Friday 4:00PM. In effect, issues flowing in from the weekend belong to the new point person.
	• Any existing issue should remain with the engineer that was on point when the issue appeared.
	• An email transferring the baton should be sent, with team included as a CC.
	
**Rotation**
It is expected that the listed engineer be on point starting on the Monday morning of their rotation and announce during first standup of the week . It is the engineer's responsibility to get coverage on days when they are unavailable. That person should email  escalation team and update calendar of said change.

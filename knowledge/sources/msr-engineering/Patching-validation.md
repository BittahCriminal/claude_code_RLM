## **How to validate patch deployment:**

patching is going to happen automatically on 3rd Monday of every month plus one manual deployment on same day to make sure everything is patched to confirm if it was succussed or failed you can navigate to [Azure update manager](![image.png](/.attachments/image-d48da985-2f4a-46f3-9593-d1ca81b94a91.png)) main page and filter the subscription to only GCRProdex1 2 and 3 and then click on history 

![image.png](/.attachments/image-acd84157-c69c-4aee-b4d9-a07cf62b1c16.png)

Under history you should be able to see the patch deployment schedule by its maintenance id in past 30 days

![image.png](/.attachments/image-20425ca5-e133-4d1d-a433-19cdfa4a774f.png)

If you click on status it gives you extensive information about each VMs and its patch deployment status and what updates were applied to each VMs

![image.png](/.attachments/image-b973c853-705a-4e2e-a5fe-86a501010885.png)

# Process for obtaining a business guest account as of July 2020.

A business guest account is appropriate for individuals collaborating with Microsoft on projects or services. They are created so these people can get access to buildings, networks or email as needed to support the collaboration efforts.

-------------------------
# STEP ONE
## Before you begin
Gather the following information for each person needing a business guest B- account.
- Legal First name
- Legal last name
- Name of employer
- Country of residence
- If US resident, get state located in (aka Sub Geography)
- Is the person physically located in the US (or will be coming soon)?
- Email address (work)
- Personal phone number
- Find out level of Microsoft access required such as network, email, or building?
- Was this person previously employed by Microsoft?
- If yes, what was their alias?
- Who will this person report to? (HeadTrax)
- Cost center (note B- accounts are not usually paid employees of Microsoft, but there may be costs associated with onboarding such as background checks, card keys, office space or other costs)
- PO number (if applicable)
- Will this person be onsite or offsite
- Workspace type (office, mobile, remote)
- Building (if applicable)
- Room (may not be applicable)
- Is an access card required for this person?
- Network domain account to be created in
- Category Standard Title - Options are: 
Business Programs & Ops
Business Operations & Program Mgmt
Business Support
Engineering
Program Management
Software Engineering
Hardware Engineering
Hardware Dev Eng

------------------------

# STEP TWO
## Background Check (aka Background Screening)
Background checks for Microsoft are handled by an outside company called HireRight. Be sure to clearly state this is for a BUSINESS GUEST account when requesting the screening. 

1. For US-based accounts - Contact Supplier Screening Program <supscrn@microsoft.com> to order background screening. Make sure you have the above information at the time the request is made.

2. For all individuals outside the US, contact External Supplier Screening <esscrng@microsoft.com>.

---------------
# STEP THREE
## HeadTrax
Once the background screening is complete, the HeadTrax entry must be completed. Within Microsoft Research please contact Stacy Grover <stacyg@microsoft.com> or the admin team with the request. 

The following information is required to complete the HeadTrax entry.
- Cost Center
- Reports to Email
- Physical Work Location (Country)
- Type of Position
- Start Date
- End Date
- Outsourced Agreement ID is N/A
- PO Number
- Category - Standard Title
- Will this person be onsite or offsite?
- Workspace Type
- Building
- Room (provided by admin)
- Country
- Sub Geography
- Is an access card required for this person?
- Network Access Type (Email/Network or Network only)
- Select Domain Information 
- First Name
- Middle Name
- Last Name
- Email Address
- Background Screen ID - This is the HireRight number provided by the background screen process. (e.g. "GA-061120-2R7MJ")
- Has this person ever worked at Microsoft? (yes/no)
- Person Alias (in case worked before at Microsoft)
- Person's Personnel Number


_Note the company will need to be listed in MyOrder as a supplier with a supplier number before the HeadTrax position can be created._

------------------------
# STEP FOUR

## **Signed Agreement between the company and Microsoft**
The signed agreement between the Business Guest’s employer and Microsoft. This may be a Joint Development Agreements, OEM/IHV/ISV contracts, or a Memoranda of Understanding. CELA does not provide a template for business guest agreements. A recent example of an accepted agreement is provided at the bottom of this page.

1. Prepare the business guest agreement. This is usually in the form of a letter between the companies. An example is provided below used in MSR in June 2020.
2. Locate your CELA contact - Go to [CELAWEB Find Contact](https://findcontact.microsoft.com/) page. This provides the name of the attorney assigned to your group. 
3. The signed agremeent does not require approval from the actual attorney. It is acceptable to locate that attorney's assigned paralegal using [Dr. Whom](https://who/) for the paralegal to review and approve.
4. Send the approved DRAFT to the business guest's company to review and approve the DRAFT
5. Once both parties agree to the language, submit the letter for formal electronic signature.
- Electronic signatures [information can be found here](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/sites/CELAWeb-Contracts-And-Licensing/SitePages/E-sign.aspx). One-off agreements can be entered [using this page](https://contracts-microsoft.azurewebsites.net/Contract/Offline/Approve-ESign). 

**Note** this agreement must be signed by a GM or higher on Microsoft's side per [this MSPolicy](https://nam06.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmicrosoft.sharepoint.com%2Fsites%2Fms_policy%2FPages%2FPolicy.aspx%3Fpolicyid%3DMSPOLICY-1319973531-24&data=02%7C01%7Cerdman%40microsoft.com%7C6903326724f943d326ba08d8187c2f36%7C72f988bf86f141af91ab2d7cd011db47%7C1%7C0%7C637286268928732527&sdata=cE%2BsFDs7m6rx%2BSQfMNDywrLf5bG8IOja5PUlFhZzljM%3D&reserved=0).

---------------
#STEP FIVE 
Up tot his point, the Headtrax positions have been "pending review" by the Business Guest Program Group (BGPG). Once the agreement is signed you should notify BGPG (reviewb-@microsoft.com) to proceed. You must provide them with the HeadTrax ID.

BGPB will review and approve the positions. The hiring manager (according to the HeadTrax entry) is then notified to approve the positions in HeadTrax. They will get an email that shows the current approval status:

__Request Details: Create Position Wizard - CS_
  
_Request ID: ########_
_Position #: ########_ 
_Start Date#: Jun 17 2020 12:00AM_ 
  
_Your HeadTrax request has been "Final Approved". The request changes should be visible in HeadTrax within 24-48 hours._

•	_All other countries, please follow your local processes for completing the necessary contractual requirements. For questions, please reach out to your HR/Finance contact._

Note: Please expect push back email, if any discrepancies in Prior MS History details provided. | If you have elected to have building/network access for your External Staff, this will be forwarded to the appropriate team(s) for further action|_

------------------
#STEP SIX
Once the hiring manager approves the HeadTrax position, the business guest will automatically be sent an RAA to sign. They will also be sent a link to complete the Supplier Code of Conduct (SCoC) training. These must be completed by the business guest before a badge/alias can be issued.

------------------
#STEP SEVEN

The hiring manager receives the network credentials and they should send the business guest a welcome email (to their current email address) and begin the onboarding process.

------------------

If you have onboarded your VMs to GCR-automation Azure patch management and want to see its current compliance status:

- You can navigate to GCR-automation account and click on update management and see the reports. Yuo can filter it by your VM name to only see the report for your VM.
![image.png](/.attachments/image-b06c9219-ca4f-470c-b162-7d2dc202fda5.png)

- If you want to see more details regarding patch status such as see which packages is installed or needs to be installed on your node, you can navigate to your VM main page in Azure and then click on Guest + host updates as follow:

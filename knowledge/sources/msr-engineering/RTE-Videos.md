## A shift in internal policy has changed this process. Now all videos are saved to OneDrive not Streams. This will require an updated process for upload.

The purpose of this page is to inform the team of our effort to ensure recorded meetings are accessible to all of RTE.

#Problem: 
- Recorded meetings are only accessible to those that were invited to the meeting
- Cross team collaborations are inhibited by not sharing information across all of RTE
- Managing individual recording memberships is a burden

#Solution:
Leverage a Microsoft Streams team to collect all RTE team videos. This gives us a single location that allows other members of RTE to stay up to date on our Programs, Projects, Meetings and Strategy. 

##Groups and Channels

[RTEVideos](https://msit.microsoftstream.com/group/df6fea7e-1c0a-4094-84ad-8fac1f21211b)- [Click to Join Group](https://outlook-sdf.office.com/owa/rtevideos@service.microsoft.com/groupsubscription.ashx?action=join&source=Exchange&guid=031aac4d-fff5-42dc-8c81-f3b5e8bf74b3) This group is intended to be RTE Internal only. 

  - [Strategy](https://msit.microsoftstream.com/channel/401ba4ff-0400-96f3-991a-f1eaee27cece) - This channel is intended for strategic videos
  - [SysEng Meetings](https://msit.microsoftstream.com/channel/9111a1ff-0400-8385-d251-f1eaed327880) - This channel is intended for our Systems Engineering Meetings
  - [Tech talks](https://msit.microsoftstream.com/channel/401ba4ff-0400-96f3-cf9f-f1eaee27dd10) - This channel is intended for our RTE Tech Talks

##Limitations
One limitation to this solution is you cannot add existing channels to the group. This is unfortunate as I would like to link preexisting channels in our group. 

##How to add talks
To ensure all members of RTE have access you will need to add each video to the group/channel. I was unable to find a way to configure our meetings to do this by default. If anybody figures this out please update these instructions.

1. Browse to your recorded video on streams
2. Click ... and select 'Add to Group/Channel'
![image.png](/.attachments/image-6d9cfc96-288e-45f5-b755-f38713abd14c.png)
3. Switch the filter to Groups and search for 'RTE Videos'
![image.png](/.attachments/image-fdfc06ef-aa2d-460e-ba14-270564ed6839.png)
4. Choose the channel and permission you want and click save

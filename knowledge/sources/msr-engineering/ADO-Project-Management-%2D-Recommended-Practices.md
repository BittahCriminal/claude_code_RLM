

> **_Note:_**  _This recommendation from MSR-ADO is for teams with multiple development teams & projects with no dependencies and complex to manage everyone on Same Board._

If your team is complex and working on multiple projects at a time with multiple sets of Teams, We recommend you to use Azure Boards with [SAFe®](https://scaledagile.com/what-is-safe/). 

#Plan your project

Depending on your project team size and needs, Azure Boards can be configured to fit and allow teams to focus only on their set of work, here is a planning effort

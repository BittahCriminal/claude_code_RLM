# Why
As opportunities arise we often find ourselves asking the question 'can we afford to do this?'. Answering this question is challenging unless we have a commitment to continuously measuring our services impact. Maintaining this key metric is another great way to ensure our investments are defensible and aligned with the divisional strategy. It also helps us understand when we need to sunset services to reduce operational toil. 

This page is to help define when and how we execute our service rationalization efforts.

# How

There are 4 major parts of the framework. Each phase feeds information to the next in which we gain better understanding our our services, their costs, and impact statements. At the end of the process we should have a list of key services that we all feel meet our impact targets. 

The 4 Phases are:

- Assessment and Discovery
- Define what stays, goes, or is unknown
- Migration/Decomm Planning
- Execution

## Assessment and Discovery

**Outcome**: A inventory of services with mapped infrastructure, dependencies, costs, and impact.

- Service Discovery​
- Infrastructure Discovery​
- Service Relationship Mapping​
- Service Management Discovery​
- Identify customers/consumers
- Identify key metrics

## What stays, what goes, what is unknown
**Outcome**: A classification on what says vs goes and things that need more investigations.

- Workload Location Design​
- Design target state Blueprint​
- Backup & DR Design​
- Policy Design​
- Security Design​
- Service Management Design

## Migration OR Decomm​ Planning
**Outcome**: Identify migration dependencies, validate infrastructure, and build a test plan.

- Confirm “move together” dependencies​
- Confirm Infrastructure-To-Application Mapping​
- Determine Testing Needs​
- Confirm Performance Requirements/Complete Benchmarking​
- Complete Release Packaging & Scheduling 
- Develop communications plan
- Asses customer guidance for alternatives

## Execution
**Outcome:** Lower impact services removed and high impact services documented.
- Develop supporting documentation
- Execute Communications plan
- Execute Migrations/Decomms
- Validate estimated budgetary impact

To pull this information directly follow these steps.

## FOR ARM EXPRESSROUTES

https://msit.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/8e0908f2-a7f6-4573-8a1e-42f524092250/ReportSection
1. Filter your selection to check MSR Labs and MSR Next under the ServiceTree Hierarchy. The results will be displayed on the bottom panel.
2. Export the results by clicking the ellipses on the far right.
3. Save the data in Excel

 
## For CLASSIC EXPRESSROUTES
Using the same report as above, access the DEDICATED CIRCUIT VNET USAGE tab at the very bottom and filter to your group again. If no classic ERs exist it will appear blank.

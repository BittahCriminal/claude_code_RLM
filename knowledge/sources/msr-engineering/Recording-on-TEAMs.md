**Beware that anyone in the lecture can hit Record** (We always suggest the host does it because the person who hits the record button is then the person who owns the recording). 

To start or stop recording, go to the _More_ drop down, select _Record and transcribe_, and select _Start recording_ (Go back to stop recording).  

![Screenshot 2023-04-25 115952.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-04-25%20115952-e365c8b4-61b8-4420-a359-4cde04611138.png)

The recording can be found in your OneDrive under recordings.

![Teams.png](/.attachments/Teams-d2426e37-6f80-4bdf-abfc-b5763837c386.png)

The recording will be hosted automatically on Stream [<A  href="https://www.office.com/launch/stream?auth=2">Stream | Microsoft 365 (office.com)</A>]().

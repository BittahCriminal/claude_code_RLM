# Why is this important?
As a team of service engineers our core competency is offering services to T&R that help the division. These services should add value that offsets their expense. When creating services we must leverage corporate tooling and processes to ensure we understand the costs and risk profile. This is to say to create a service you must onboard it properly. The below will help you understand how we do this in our team.

# What is the scope of this page
If the need has any operational overhead (people hours), infrastructure, or code please continue.

# How

## Manager approval and Service document
Please propose the service by sending your manager an email with the below information. They will review and if approved we will start the onboarding process which starts with the [service definition template](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/:f:/t/TnREng7/EsBaxZjsaSRAiJsA2TwFPiQB-EXBHd1y6WhgKNMhyebK7A?e=XGsRVQ). 

In your service brief:

- Executive summary of value to the division
- Key metrics
- Executive Pitch Slide
- Primary/Backup Owner(s)
- Infrastructure required (Azure Subscription, ADO Repo, ?)
- Documented on our wiki
- Estimated costs of infrastructure
- Estimated operational overhead (in person hours per month)
- Estimated lifetime of service
- Intake for customer feedback [Team Standard](https://dev.azure.com/msresearch/MSR%20Engineering/_wiki/wikis/MSR-Engineering.wiki/4893/Customer-Feedback)
- Be reviewed in our operational review meetings
- Monitoring
- Communication plan for outages/updates

## Service Tree
Once approved it will need appear in our [Shared Engineering Services](https://servicetree.msftcloudes.com/main.html#/OrganizationModel/TeamGroup/Service/4cc1ba9e-5c68-4624-b65f-add9fae85866) Service Tree Service Group. Please identify who will send email to MSR-Green to execute this addition.

## Monthly Operational Reviews
Monthly reviews will be performed to ensure that our services have an appropriate cost benefit ratio. Those that don't will be reviewed for adjustments to improve the ratio. Please see our [SES ROB calendar](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/teams/AIRDEPOT/Lists/SES%20ROB/calendar.aspx?InitialTabId=Ribbon.Calendar.Events&VisibilityContext=WSSListAndLibrary&OR=Teams-HL&CT=1630347760326) for timing. If you're a new service owner please contact Trevor to have you added to the meeting. 

## Compliance
Each service is responsible for actioning any RCT submissions and reviews to ensure we're protecting Microsoft and its reputation. Being an internal team means that many of our services do not meet the bar for RCT but we should be aware of when we may engage. Below is a link to their scoping document and a snippet of the relevant section. See Trevor if you have any questions. 

```When should you engage with the Compliance process?

Follow the T&R Release and Compliance process whenever you plan to:
- Access, onboard, collect, share, store, or release data

  - Examples: Customer or employee data

- Share or release projects outside of your team

   - Examples: apps, source code, datasets, APIs, websites

- Host Events or send bulk communications

   - Examples: Newsletters, Announcements

- Hand off a project to a T&R Research Intern

- Conduct a research study or survey
```

# Service details

**Service Name:** Customer Experience Program (CXP)
**Service Description:** 
This service is internal facing to RTE. It endeavors to capture an understanding of our customer's needs, and to qualify their level of satisfaction with each engagement they have with us.

## Service Requirements
- Time and expertise to establish OKRs and/or KPIs for each service leveraging the CXP service to capture customer information and feedback.
- Time to request, collect, collate, analyze, and summarize customer needs.
- Time to request, collect, collate, analyze, and summarize customer feedback (potentially before, and) after engagements.

# Costs
- Infrastructure costs per month
  - $0/month
- SES hours per month
  - Initial outreach ~ 10 hr
  - Ongoing feedback requests and processing ~ 10 hr 
- Risks 
   - The risks are more related to *not* doing this work, where we will continue to position our services based on what we assume our customers need, rather than on what they say they need. We'll continue to deliver services and engagements based on how we think we should, rather than how our customers want us to.

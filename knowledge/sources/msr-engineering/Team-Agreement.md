# Meetings
- Reinforce meeting standards ([CHARMS](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/:p:/r/teams/FutureofRemoteWorkInitiative/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8AC91600-F0CE-448A-B543-A94953C04936%7D&file=CHARM%20slides.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true&DefaultItemOpen=1&wdLOR=c0CB0D82D-D479-49E5-8D24-8D6945057741&cid=41868b7e-eeec-44bc-bca6-b27c8d5dd731)) and to Accept or Decline meetings.
- Decline all meetings ahead of PTO and cancelling where appropriate.
- Start all meetings 5 minutes after the half hour.
- Record meetings so people can catch up if unable to attend.
- Holding regular Leadership meetings and holding any additional focus meetings when attendees are available,
- Support each other and productively challenge when appropriate.
- Make every effort to not schedule Leadership  meetings on Fridays.


# Collaboration
- Clearly define action item owners and agreed priority.
- Define/Validate scope of processes i.e., RTE or Team (line of autonomy).
- To bring to the larger team new work that has a broad scope or touch on more than one team for prioritization and work assignment.
- Undertake, that when a problem poses a broad impact or risk, the issue is agreed and discussed with Leadership before a solution is actioned.
- Assure we are making every effort to both think about, include and communicate to everyone our actions impact.

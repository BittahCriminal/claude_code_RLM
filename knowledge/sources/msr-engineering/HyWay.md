> In the HyWay project at MSR India, we've taken a stab at addressing a small but vital challenge in the hybrid workplace --- enabling informal, unstructured hallway-style conversations between physical/local and virtual/remote users. Think of the hallway at a conference or the water cooler at an office.


## In Person Attendees
Those of you who will be attending this event in person will observe displays setup in the room. 
- Please hang around the displays to interact with the remote participants.
- These are the `conversation zones` where you will get to mingle with remote users joining via HyWay. 
> Please note that conversations on Hyway are not recorded.

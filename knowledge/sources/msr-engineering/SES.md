# Background
Research Technology Engineering has been supporting our division for almost 30 years. Over these years we have been able to adapt to the changing environment (internet, cloud) and maintain a high level of impact. We are a group of individuals with diverse backgrounds that align in wanting to make a difference in the world. We all recognize the privilege of our role and aim to delight customers with every engagement.

# Mission
Our mission is to ensure the reliability, scalability, and efficiency of the research department's infrastructure and services, enabling our scientists to focus on advancing scientific knowledge and discovery. 

To achieve this, we will:
1. Build and maintain resilient and scalable systems and services that can handle the demands of modern scientific research.
2. Automate repetitive and error-prone tasks to reduce manual effort and increase efficiency.
3. Continuously monitor and improve system performance, availability, and security to ensure maximum uptime and data integrity.
4. Collaborate closely with other teams to ensure seamless integration, interoperability and alignment of systems and services.
5. Foster a culture of innovation, experimentation, and continuous learning to stay ahead of emerging technologies and best practices.

# Scope of impact 
Our primary focus is to accelerate research in T&R. We occasionally invest in other division's services when they align with our unique skills and have the support of our leadership.

# Services

When helping our customers we need to identify the balance between **Resilience Services** and **Adaptive Solutions**. Think of these as opposite ends of a slider, not checkboxes. In research each project could be at a different phase of development. They could just be a spark of an idea or preparing to graduate to a product group. We also have a strong commitment to **Delivery Excellence**. This service helps us **shape, deliver and report** on our efforts. 

Below is a high-level framing of how we manage our portfolio. Each will (eventually) link to more information. There are defined Leaders for each area who have made commitments to helping the team shape and report on impact. You can find more details on these roles in our [Leadership page](https://dev.azure.com/msresearch/MSR%20Engineering/_wiki/wikis/MSR-Engineering.wiki/5298/Leadership).

SLA's
    Pick up all the urgent escalations within the 1-hour SLA window.
    Have 90% of our urgent escalations done within 1 business day.
    Have 100% urgent escalations closed within 2 days.
    No urgent escalation cases should exceed 3 days in open status.
    Cases in escalation should have daily follow up.
    After three days, it needs to be closed or downgraded to a de-escalated case

Adding the account MSR Virtual Lectures to your Outlook client.
Here’s how to do it:
 
In Outlook click File, and then you’ll see this: Click Add Account.
![6.jpg](/.attachments/6-66ccc04a-3e14-4bd8-aaf4-8d22982e033f.jpg)


After that you’ll see a window like this: type in vlecture@microsoft.com and hit Connect.
Screenshot email address is incorrect. Please use vlecture@microsoft.com.
![7.jpg](/.attachments/7-33d65c6e-4215-4493-b22b-852630f789be.jpg)



It will ask you who you want to sign in as, and you should choose current user (in other words it will use your MS creds). You have been given full perms on the new account.
 
That should be all you need to do. It will add MSR Virtual Lectures as a folder/inbox but you won’t ever need to look in there:

![8.jpg](/.attachments/8-9b3e4646-c0af-4354-8a37-eadfd29f86ba.jpg)


But now you’ll have a new calendar from which you should send all virtual-only lecture invites.

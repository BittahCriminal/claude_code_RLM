Summer poster session for Paul Bennett using Hyway. Ajay / Paul will be POC for Redmond. Event will consist of attendees and presenters, that may be either physical/virtually attending and presenting.

TBD on whether we'll know who will be in person vs. virtual but the aspiration is the Hyway experience allows participants of either environment to have as close of an experience regardless.

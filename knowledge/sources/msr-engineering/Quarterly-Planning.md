The Quarterly Planning spreadsheet is meant to be a plan for a quarter plan.  

**Why:**  
With our heavy workload, we are constantly getting new work given to us, and we need a way to plan and prioritize our work. In the past we have had projects that got started, but then dropped and some were never completed. Leadership would like a way to make more informed decisions on project prioritization and starting with a plan will provide insight into what we believe our priorities are at the start of the quarter. Having a quarterly plan will also help our Engineering teams understand what Leadership is expecting for the quarter. 

**How:** 
The PM team will partner with leadership to fill out the spreadsheet on a quarterly basis.  

The GCR Program Manager and Leader will work closely with the GCR team to identify all GCR projects that are planned to be worked on in the quarter we are planning for.  

The Governance Program Manager and Leader will work with the Governance team to identify all Governance projects that are planned to be worked on in the quarter we are planning for.  

For the Dev team, the Dev Manager will partner with the Dev PM, respective product stakeholders and the dev team to identify the work for the quarter we are planning for.

For the Media team, the Media manager will partner with the Media team to identify the work for the quarter we are planning for.

The future goal that we are working towards will be to have all projects planned before the start of the quarter. This will help to set priority and to assign people to the projects.

Once the spreadsheet has been agreed to by Leadership and “locked” there should be no adding or removing projects. Notes can be updated, but the point is to be able to compare what we planned versus what we are actually working on. 

The PM assigned to each project will be responsible for making sure their project is in ADO. 

**Qtrly Planning Spreadsheet Columns:** 

**Title/ADO Link:** This will be the title in ADO for the Epic and a link to the Epic

**State:** This will be the state in ADO and is a drop down in the spreadsheet

**Qtrly milestones/features:** This will be the monthly deliverables that are planned to be achieved.

**Assigned To** Who is assigned to the Epic in ADO

**Process:** a drop down Full or Low

**Outcome:** This will be used at the end of the quarter to identify if we met our plan or not.

**Comments:** This will be used to identify what kept us from meeting our plan, or any additional work that was done

Dates:
**1st day** of the quarter: Review the previous Qtrly planning and report out what was/was not complete and why
**1st Friday** of every month: Review the Qtrly planning to going into next Qrt
**15th of the last month** in the Qtr - Qtrly Plan ready for review
**22nd of the last month**: All teams have reviewed and agreed to the plan
**Last day** of the Qtr: Plan is "Locked" and no changes are made going forward

Desired Outcomes:
To be able to identify the upcoming work.
To be able to identify who is working on what.
To be able to identify what gets completed vs. what was planned.
right sizing the work we are doing with the resources we have available and therefore setting ourselves up for success.

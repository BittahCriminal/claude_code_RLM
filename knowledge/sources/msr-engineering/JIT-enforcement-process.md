LETS GET STARTED 

navigate to https://ms.portal.azure.com/#home and select the **Microsoft Entra Privileged** Icon if it is not present you may need to search for it

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131848.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131848-4dd5cb48-a57b-4373-bd4d-8e8ea473de36.png)

You should be brought to this page
 
![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131042.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131042-997e07fe-0309-43be-bb90-f4e1b0ae35be.png)

From here you will need to click on **Groups** in the left hand column

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131133.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131133-c28b4ce1-e44e-4dda-93bc-7278834c3a16.png)

enter the PIM group you are wanting to access and click into the group once it shows in the list 

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131207.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131207-a74ef6e0-1022-40ab-9f8d-4c2936b62a1a.png)

First click on **Roles** 
Then click on **Member** or **Owner** 
Most often you will only need to be a **Member**; **if you dont know ask** do not assume you need owner status 

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131251.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131251-60db81c5-8bc7-4ea0-a1b8-f73a34d471d0.png) 

Once you click Member you will see who is in the group under **Active Assignments** 

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 141809.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20141809-cf92fc5b-a492-49d4-96c6-7d77cef546ee.png)

From here you will click on  **+ Add Assignments**

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131316.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131316-857edff2-2206-420b-a7ba-b4d8c4872db9.png)

You will be brought to this page 

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131332.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131332-e833e9f9-b7af-4597-89e9-0078ed39a803.png)

click on **No member selected**

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131332.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131332-24b5ff73-9d21-4eda-ad93-ea5b7b55e05b.png)

There will be a pop up allowing you to search for yourself; and click **Select** 

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131403.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131403-1712254b-4a2b-4829-aeeb-9fef0013b2ee.png)

You should now see your name in the list of **Select members**

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131451.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131451-288b8079-8568-4883-8d13-bc9aae5d3396.png)

choose **Setting** and **Active** 
Fill in the appropriate **time needed and justification and click Assign** 
**minimum activation is 5 minutes** 

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131527.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131527-7bd49e1a-157f-49e2-822e-7f770031d5f1.png)

You should see yourself in the group once it processes your request 

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131553.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131553-fc86c623-aec4-4c27-8235-792148f560a0.png)

ADO sync does take a little bit of time to update; I find if you go into ADO and refresh you will see yourself immediately 

![Screenshot 2023-12-06 131632.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202023-12-06%20131632-f257282f-15ed-418d-aeea-89bfdc2162d8.png)

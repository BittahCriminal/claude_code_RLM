Welcome to the MSR Engineering Wiki.

The scope of this wiki is to provide information for members of our Research Technology Engineering (RTE) team.  That said, the project is readable by any authenticated user in Microsoft.  The intent of this is that some of the information could be useful to other engineering teams.

Our primary function is to augment corporate services to enable efficient execution of Research.  We strive to keep apprised of technological advancements and provide engineering excellence across a wide range of services.

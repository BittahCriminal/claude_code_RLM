##Background
Effective July 1 2018, the MSResearch ADO collection has been established as the primary security-compliant managed instance for all research and incubation teams. The operations team will be working with development teams to get compliant and consolidate Azure DevOps (formerly Visual Studio Online (VS) and Visual Studio Team Services (VSTS) prior to that) accounts in the Research and Incubation organization. This is directly aligned to the AI+R Security compliance requirements for all code releases to get compliant to their requirements and be centralized in the single managed company repository. 

##ADO Support Operations Charter 
The T&R ADO Support (**mailto:MSR-ADO**) team has been established under the T&R Support Services organization umbrella to provide central ADO support services to all research and incubation teams. The primary goal of this support service team is to ensure that all requested Azure Devops projects and code repositories are managed for their entire project lifecycle in accordance with the requirements of the company and the T&R Security compliance team’s requirements. The MSResearch ADO collection has been identified as the primary destination account in scope for Research and Incubation teams to use for all active Microsoft Research and Incubation projects.
 
##Support Service Scope
Microsoft Visual Studio Online / Azure DevOps projects and collections outside MSResearch and part of the T&R organization. 

##Support Service Goals
1. **Collection administration:** To provide centralized ADO collection organizational level access and authorization support and project lifecycle management of MSResearch and sub-projects. 

2. **Extension management**: To provide support for evaluating new ADO extensions. The focus is on security and impact across the ADO organization. Our goal is to ensure we're not approving extensions that could leak sensitive intellectual property. 

3. **Escalation Support**: To provide assistance escalating issues we do not have the capacity or capability to support. 

4. **Corporate Tooling and Policy Onboarding**: To stay connected with the development community in Microsoft and adopt scalable solutions that help us secure our codebases and increase developer velocity.

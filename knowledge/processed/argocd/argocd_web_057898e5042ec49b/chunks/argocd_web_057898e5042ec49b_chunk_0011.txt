## Tips¶

Use a service likeGrammarlyto check your grammar.

Having your computer read text out loud is a way to catch problems, e.g.:

- Word substitutions (i.e. the wrong word is used, but spelled.
correctly).
- Sentences that do not read correctly will sound wrong.
On Mac, to set-up:

- Go toSystem Preferences / Accessibility / Spoken Content.

```
System Preferences / Accessibility / Spoken Content
```

- Choose a System Voice (I likeSiri Voice 1).

```
Siri Voice 1
```

- EnableSpeak selection.

```
Speak selection
```

To hear text, select the text you want to hear, then press option+escape.

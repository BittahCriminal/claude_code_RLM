Eurasian jays use a wide range of visual displays and calls for communication, and are are prodigious mimics: their song may include all the sounds they have ever heard, including crying babies, the songs of other birds, and even the sounds of lawn mowers. The current conservation status of the Eurasian jay is “Least Concern.” Many of the animals on O’Reilly covers are endangered; all of them are important to the world.

The common pandora is a popular food in many Mediterranean countries. While the IUCN categorizes this fish as a species of least concern, the Archipelagos Institute of Marine Conservation urges pandora enthusiasts to “Consume Responsibly” by choosing sustainably sized fish and avoiding consumption during the breeding season. Many of the animals on O’Reilly covers are endangered; all of them are important to the world.
